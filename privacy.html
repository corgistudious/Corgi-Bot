Corgi-Bot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Corgi Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, stores, and protects information when you use Corgi-Bot, our Discord application.

By using Corgi-Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Process

Corgi-Bot may process information provided through Discord that is necessary to operate enabled bot features.

This information may include:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs
  • Channel IDs
  • Role IDs
  • Discord usernames or display names when required for a feature
  • Server configuration and bot settings
  • XP, level, ranking, and message activity information
  • Moderation records such as warnings or moderation case information
  • Ticket-related information
  • Event, contest, voting, survey, or giveaway information when those features are used
  • Other information users intentionally submit through Corgi-Bot features

2. Message Information

Some Corgi-Bot features may temporarily process message information when necessary to provide functionality such as XP tracking, anti-spam detection, moderation, commands, tickets, events, or other server features.

Corgi-Bot does not sell Discord message content.

Message content is not intended to be permanently stored unless storage is necessary for a specific enabled feature, such as ticket records, moderation records, event submissions, or another feature that clearly requires the submitted information.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Corgi-Bot may be used to:

  • Provide and operate bot functionality.
  • Maintain server-specific configuration.
  • Provide XP, levels, rankings, and progression features.
  • Provide moderation and anti-spam functionality.
  • Operate ticket and support systems.
  • Operate events, contests, voting, surveys, and giveaways.
  • Diagnose errors and maintain service reliability.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Improve Corgi-Bot and develop new functionality.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Data Storage

Corgi-Bot may use databases and hosting infrastructure to store information necessary for its functionality.

Stored information may include server settings, user progression data, moderation records, ticket information, and other information required by enabled features.

We take reasonable measures designed to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

Corgi Studio does not sell personal information collected through Corgi-Bot.

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers when necessary to operate Corgi-Bot, such as hosting or database providers.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security and integrity of Corgi-Bot.

6. Discord

Corgi-Bot operates using Discord's platform and APIs. Discord processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Corgi Studio does not control Discord's independent data processing practices.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Corgi-Bot functionality, maintain security and service integrity, resolve disputes, enforce applicable agreements, or satisfy legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature for which it is used.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of eligible information associated with their Discord account by contacting Corgi Studio.

Server owners or authorized administrators may also contact us regarding eligible data associated with their Discord server.

To help identify relevant records, a deletion request may need to include the applicable Discord User ID or Server ID.

Requests can be sent to: corgistudious@gmail.com

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed by Corgi-Bot. However, no online system or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Corgi-Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies. We do not knowingly use Corgi-Bot to collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord under applicable requirements.

11. Third-Party Services

Corgi-Bot may depend on third-party services such as Discord, hosting infrastructure, and database services. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Corgi-Bot, applicable requirements, or our data practices change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.
